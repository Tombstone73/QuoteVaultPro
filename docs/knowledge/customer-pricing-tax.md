# Customer pricing tiers and sales tax

Customers are tenant-owned business accounts. Contacts are tenant-owned people and may be standalone or linked to one or more Customer accounts through active relationship records. When a selected Contact belongs to a Customer, the Customer remains the business owner and the selected Contact remains the contact.

Core Customer create/profile/commercial updates and Contact create/person updates use the same canonical operations for the UI and reviewed Assistant plans. Customer merge, import, archive, notes, credit transactions, relationship link/unlink/status/primary changes, portal access, and production-folder references remain UI-only.

Customer pricing and tax are resolved through saved Customer and organization configuration. Do not infer commercial terms or tax treatment from a company or product name. A skill can explain these records, but it does not grant authority to change them.
